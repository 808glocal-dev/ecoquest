FAQ

Common questions

How do you price your services?

Pricing is flexible and negotiable: per hour, per task, per word or per placement. We adjust to your budget and offer volume discounts as your Korean team grows.

Do candidates need to speak English?

Not necessarily. Our bilingual project managers translate guidelines, answer questions and handle daily communication, so you can work with top Korean experts who aren't fluent in English. For roles that need English, we screen for it.

How does your first-round test work?

We build a short test around your requirements: skills, domain knowledge, Korean writing quality and guideline accuracy. Only candidates who pass are introduced to you.

Can you sign our NDA and work with our vendor portal?

Yes. We are a registered Korean company and can sign NDAs, complete supplier registration and invoice in USD.

What if a candidate drops out?

If someone leaves within the first 30 days, we replace them at no extra cost.

What time zone do you work in?

Seoul (KST, UTC+9). We cover Asia-Pacific business hours and overlap with Europe and US evenings for calls.

Looking for Korean talent?

Tell us what you need. We usually reply within one business day (KST).
